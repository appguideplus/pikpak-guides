## Two different kinds of saving

**Save to the cloud** — Bring a file from a link into your PikPak account. Completing this step does not put a copy on your phone or computer.

**Download to your device** — Copy a file from PikPak to your phone or computer. Check the destination folder and available space on that device.

## Choose where to start

- **Computer:** Open the web drive to work with files without installing a separate app.
- **Android:** Use the PikPak app to access and play your files. Menu positions may differ by app version.
- **iPhone and iPad:** The iOS app cannot add download links. Save the link through the web drive, then open the file in the app using the same account.

The web version cannot download a whole folder. Download its files individually or use a PikPak desktop app.

## If something does not work

### The file is missing in the app

Check that the web and app use the same account, refresh the file list and confirm that the save task has finished.

### The link will not save

Check that the link still works, the source is available and your account has storage and transfer quota left. The source itself can delay or prevent a cloud save.

### The cloud task finished, but downloads are slow

Cloud saving and downloading to your device are separate transfers. Check your connection, remaining transfer quota and the conditions of your account.

### I cannot find a downloaded file

Open its location from the browser’s download history or PikPak’s download list. A phone download does not necessarily appear in Photos.
